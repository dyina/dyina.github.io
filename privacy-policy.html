5. Data We Handle

5.1 Health and fitness data (step counts)

Apps marked in the table above read your step counts with your permission, from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), or from the device’s built-in step sensor when those are unavailable. They read daily step totals only, and may read your past step history once, with your permission, to show your total. They do not read other health data and do not write to Health Connect or Apple Health.

  • Use: to show the distance you have walked, records, and badges. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or profiling, and we do not sell it.
  • Storage and sharing: stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to our servers (these apps have no account or backend) and is not shared with anyone, including advertising providers.
  • Background: steps are read when you open the app, not in the background.

5.2 Location

Apps marked in the table may read your location once, only when you tap a button such as “Use my current location”, with the precise or approximate permission you choose. It is used to set a starting point, stored only on your device, and not sent anywhere. The apps do not track your location in the background.

5.3 Advertising

Free versions of our apps show ads through advertising providers: Google AdMob (Google LLC) and Liftoff (Liftoff Mobile, Inc.), as shown in the table. These providers may automatically collect and process your advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device information, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalize ads. Health, fitness and precise location data are not provided to them.

  • Consent: where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the apps show a consent form before ads, and you can change your choice later from the app’s settings.
  • Opt out of personalization: Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization. iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
  • Details: Google Advertising Policy, Google Privacy Policy, Liftoff Privacy Policy.

5.4 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn whether a purchase was made.

5.5 Website and contact

  • Information you send via contact forms or email (name, email address, message)
  • Server logs (IP address, browser type, access date and time)

  • Responding to inquiries and providing support
  • Operating, maintaining, and improving our services
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements (section 5.3)

7. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The target age of each app follows its store listing. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us (section 13); we will verify and delete it as required by law.

8. Data Retention

Data stored by an app on your device stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Other personal information is kept only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law, then deleted or anonymized.

9. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not provide personal information to third parties except:

  • When we have your consent
  • When required by law
  • To the advertising providers and payment platforms listed in section 5, to the extent necessary to show ads and process purchases

10. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical, administrative, and physical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

11. Your Rights

Under APPI, you may request disclosure, correction, deletion, or suspension of use of your personal information. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, and withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. You can also revoke app permissions at any time in your device settings (including Health Connect), reset stored data in the app, or uninstall the app. Send requests to the contact below.

12. Policy Changes

We may revise this policy as necessary. Changes take effect when posted here, with the date updated above. Changes to the data an app handles will be reflected in section 4 and 5 before the change is released.

13. Contact

5. 扱うデータ

5.1 健康・フィットネスのデータ（歩数）

上の表で「あり」のアプリは、ユーザーの許可を得て、ヘルスコネクト（Android）または Apple ヘルスケア（iOS）から、これらが使えない場合は端末内蔵の歩数センサーから、歩数を読み取ります。読み取るのは1日ごとの歩数の合計だけで、許可を得た場合に限り、これまでの歩数の履歴を1度だけ読み取って総距離を表示します。ほかの健康データは読み取らず、ヘルスコネクト・Apple ヘルスケアへの書き込みもしません。

  • 利用目的：歩いた距離・記録・バッジを表示するためです。広告・解析・プロファイリングには使わず、販売もしません。
  • 保存と共有：端末内のアプリ専用の領域にだけ保存します。当方のサーバーへは送信せず（これらのアプリにはアカウントもバックエンドもありません）、広告事業者を含む第三者とも共有しません。
  • バックグラウンド：歩数はアプリを開いた時に読み取り、バックグラウンドでは読み取りません。

5.2 位置情報

表で「あり」のアプリは、「現在地を使う」などのボタンを押した時だけ、ユーザーが選んだ許可（正確またはおおよそ）に基づいて位置を1回読み取ることがあります。出発点を設定するために使い、端末内にだけ保存し、どこにも送信しません。バックグラウンドで位置を追跡することはありません。

  • 同意：必要な地域（EEA・英国など）では、広告の前に同意画面を表示し、後からアプリの設定で選択を変更できます。
  • パーソナライズの停止：Android：設定 → Google → 広告 → 広告のパーソナライズをオプトアウト。iOS：設定 → プライバシー → トラッキング。
